About Sringaara

Sringaara, as the term denotes, is for adornment. It is for the woman who loves to dress up and look beautiful, the modern woman in search of unique ways to style herself.

Our mission is to showcase an ensemble of ethnic wear and accessories from the various cultures of India to all the women living in the UAE who appreciate it. These are handpicked from the length and breadth of India, after careful selection, to suit the taste of the modern woman.

It is also an attempt to popularise the inimitable craftsmanship of Indian artisans, be it block printing, weaving or jewellery making.
